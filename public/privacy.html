Hineni — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 July 2026

Hineni (“the app”) is First Love Church’s internal attendance tool. It records whether church leaders were physically present at church events. It is used only by First Love Church leaders and administrators — it is not a public service and has no advertising.

Information we collect

  • Identity and church role — your name, email address, phone number, title, and church assignments (e.g. which Bacenta or Council you lead). This comes from First Love Church’s existing membership directory; the app does not create new identity records.
  • Location — when you check in to an event, your device’s GPS position is used to confirm you are inside the venue’s geofence at that moment. Location is not tracked in the background and is not used outside of check-in and event administration.
  • Camera — used to scan event QR codes, and (optionally) for Face ID check-in. Camera frames are processed on your device only. No photo or video is ever stored or transmitted.
  • Face template (optional) — if you enrol in Face ID check-in, a numeric face descriptor (128 numbers — not an image, and not reversible into one) is stored in your member profile. Enrolment is optional; QR and PIN check-in work without it. You may ask an administrator to delete your stored descriptor at any time.
  • Device identifier — a device fingerprint is recorded at check-in to prevent one phone from checking in multiple people.
  • Attendance records — the event, time, and method of each check-in, and administrator actions in an audit log.

How we use it

  • To record and report attendance at First Love Church events.
  • To prevent fraudulent check-ins (remote, duplicated, or impersonated).
  • To let authorised church administrators view attendance reports for the church units they oversee.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties for their own use.
  • We do not show ads or use data for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not track your location outside of the check-in flow.

Storage and processors

Data is stored with the following infrastructure providers acting as processors on the church’s behalf: Supabase (database), Vercel (hosting), and Amazon Web Services (authentication). Access is restricted by role — leaders see their own units; administrators see the units they oversee.

Retention and deletion

Attendance records are retained as part of the church’s ministry records. To correct your details, delete a stored face descriptor, or request account removal, contact your church administrator or reach us at the address below.
